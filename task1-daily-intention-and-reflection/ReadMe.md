# Daily Intention & Reflection

A simple, private journal to set your daily intention and reflect each evening.  
Helps you stay mindful, track your mood, and build a meaningful routine.

## Features

- **Morning intention** – set a focus for the day and choose your starting mood.
- **Evening reflection** – write about your day and rate it (1–5 stars).
- **History timeline** – see all past entries, including mood and rating.
- **Streak counter** – tracks how many consecutive days you’ve used the app.

## How to use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser (no server needed).
2. Go to the **Today** tab.
3. Write your intention for the day and click **Save Intention**.
4. In the evening, scroll down to **Evening reflection** – write your thoughts, tap a star rating, and click **Save Reflection**.
5. Switch to the **History** tab to see all your past entries.

## Data storage

All entries are saved in your browser’s `localStorage`.  
Your data stays on your device – nothing is sent anywhere.
